Frequently asked questions

How do I connect a bank?

On the Accounts screen, tap Add → Connect a Bank. Create a setup token at your SimpleFIN server (or the SimpleFIN Bridge), then paste it into Cairn. The token is single-use; Cairn exchanges it for an access URL and stores that in your Keychain.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in a private database. If iCloud Sync is on, it is stored in your own private iCloud database with end-to-end encrypted financial fields. Cairn has no server and cannot see your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Cairn work with Apple Card or Apple Savings?

Yes. Apple Card and Apple Savings are not reliably available through aggregators, so Cairn imports their CSV exports into a manual account. Create a manual account (Add → Add Manual Account), open it, and choose Import CSV.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Data. This removes local data and, if sync is enabled, your data in iCloud. You can also revoke Cairn's access at your SimpleFIN Bridge.

Is Cairn open source?

Yes. The source is available at github.com/sehejjain/cairn under the Apache-2.0 license.

Cairn is not affiliated with SimpleFIN, Apple, or any financial institution.
